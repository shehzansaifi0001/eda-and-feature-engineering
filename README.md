## 🔍 Projects

### 1. Google Play Store – EDA & Feature Engineering

**Objective:** Explore Google Play Store applications to understand app distribution, ratings, reviews, pricing, and category-wise trends.

**Key Activities:**

* Data cleaning and handling missing values.
* Analysing app categories and ratings.
* Exploring relationships between app size, installs, price, and ratings.
* Creating visualisations to identify patterns.
* Performing feature engineering to prepare data for further analysis.

### 2. Flight Price – Feature Engineering

**Objective:** Explore flight booking data and transform raw features into meaningful variables for analysis and potential predictive modelling.

**Key Activities:**

* Handling date and duration columns.
* Extracting journey day, month, and other useful information.
* Converting flight duration into total minutes.
* Handling categorical and numerical features.
* Preparing the dataset for Machine Learning.

### 3. Red Wine Quality – EDA

**Objective:** Analyse physicochemical properties of red wine and understand their relationship with wine quality.

**Key Activities:**

* Analysing numerical features and their distributions.
* Exploring correlations between chemical properties.
* Investigating relationships between features and wine quality.
* Identifying class imbalance in the target variable.
* Exploring data visualisation techniques.
